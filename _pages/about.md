Welcome to my webpage ! I am a postdoctoral researcher in the numerical analysis group at the <a href="https://www.unige.ch/math/" target="_blank">University of Geneva</a>. My research develops numerical methods for large-scale forward and inverse problems governed by PDEs, bridging mathematics and industrial applications. 

I obtained my PhD in applied mathematics from the University of Lorraine and the University of Liège in 2022. After that, I held research positions at Siemens Digital Industries Software and within the <a href="https://seiscope2.osug.fr/?lang=en" target="_blank">Seiscope</a> consortium at the University of Grenoble Alpes.

***

### Research interests
- Wave propagation & Helmholtz problems (ABC, PML)
- Domain decomposition methods
- High-performance computing
- Full waveform inversion (FWI)
- Bayesian uncertainty quantification
- Gradient flows in probability spaces
